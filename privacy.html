Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-21

This policy explains how Varyos Weld ("we", "Varyos Weld") handles personal data on the varyosweld.com website and in the Varyos Weld calculator app (the "Service"). Varyos Weld is primarily a local, on-device calculator. Account registration is optional. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

1. Data controller

Varyos Weld is operated by an individual developer based in the Republic of Türkiye (Muhammet Yiğit, the seller shown on the App Store listing), who is the data controller for the data described below. For any privacy question, including access, correction, or deletion requests, contact team@varyosweld.com.

2. What data we process

  • Website launch-list email: if you leave your email address on the website to be notified at launch, only your email address is recorded (in a Google Form / Google Sheet).
  • Registration data (optional): if you register in the app, your name and email are collected. Your name is kept on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere. Your email is submitted to a Google Form (so we can send a welcome email) and is also used as the identifier for your cloud-synced saved calculations.
  • Presets and saved calculations: joint presets and the technical values you save (dimensions, process settings, deposition rates, material choices) are stored locally on your device by default. If you register with an email, your saved presets are also synced to a cloud backend (a Google Apps Script / Google Sheet) keyed by your email, so the same account can retrieve them on another device. On their own these technical values do not identify you, but they are stored in association with your account.
  • On-device storage: the app uses local storage on your device (for example localStorage in the web version) to remember your session, preferences, and saved data. This data is not automatically sent to our servers.
  • PDF reports: reports you generate are created and shared directly from your device using the native share/print sheet. Nothing is uploaded to a server as part of generating a report. Reports are kept on the device in the in-app Saved Reports list.
  • Subscription and purchase data: in-app purchases (the Premium subscription, which unlocks PDF report export and the AI assistant) are processed through the app store and RevenueCat, which manages subscription and purchase status for the app using an anonymous identifier generated by its SDK, according to its own standard practices. We do not receive your payment details, and we do not send your email or name to RevenueCat.
  • AI assistant messages: see the next section.

3. In-app AI assistant

The app has an optional AI assistant that answers questions about the app's own calculators. Before first use, the app tells you which service processes your messages and what is sent, and asks for your explicit permission ("Agree & continue" or "Not now"). Nothing is sent until you agree. You can withdraw this permission at any time from the Account screen. If you use the assistant:

  • The text of your conversation so far (your messages and the assistant's earlier replies in that session) and your app language are sent over HTTPS to a small proxy service that we operate on Cloudflare Workers. That proxy forwards the conversation to Cloudflare Workers AI, which runs the Meta Llama 3.3 70B large language model on Cloudflare's infrastructure, to generate the reply. The assistant does not automatically receive your calculation inputs, saved data, email address, or name — only what you type into the chat. Please do not share sensitive personal information in the chat.
  • The conversation is kept in memory in the app and is not saved between sessions. We do not store the content of your messages ourselves. Cloudflare processes the requests as our service provider; according to Cloudflare's Workers AI privacy terms, prompts and replies are not stored by Cloudflare beyond processing the request and are not used to train models.
  • To limit abuse and protect free usage limits, the proxy counts requests per a random identifier that the app generates and stores on your device, per IP address, and overall. These counters are short-lived (they expire after the hourly or daily window, at most 24 hours) and are not linked to your email or name. Our legal basis for this is our legitimate interest in preventing abuse of the service.

4. What the app does not do

  • Account creation is optional — the app is usable as a guest, with your presets and saved calculations staying on your device. Third-party services are involved only as described above: the AI assistant (if you use it), purchases, and RevenueCat, which the app contacts at launch using an anonymous ID to check subscription status. The web calculator at /app/ also loads its rendering engine and fonts from Google's gstatic CDN.
  • No analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs, and no crash-reporting SDKs.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

5. Purposes and legal basis

We use personal data to:

  • send launch and product-update emails and the welcome email you request,
  • store your saved presets against your account and sync them across your devices,
  • provide the AI assistant and manage subscriptions/purchases,
  • maintain and improve the Service, fix errors, and provide support,
  • meet legal obligations.

Under Turkish Law No. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data ("KVKK"), Article 5, we rely on your explicit consent (for example the consent you give when you sign up for launch emails or create an account) and, where applicable, on what is necessary to provide the service you requested.

If the EU/EEA or UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applies to you, our legal bases are: performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) for account and preset sync, the AI assistant and subscriptions; your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) for launch and product-update emails and for sending messages to the AI assistant; and our legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for abuse prevention (rate limiting) and keeping the Service secure. You can withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing.

6. Service providers and international transfers

We rely on: Google (Google Forms, Google Sheets, Google Apps Script) for email signup and account/preset sync; Resend for sending emails; RevenueCat and the app store (Apple App Store) for subscriptions and purchases; and Cloudflare (Workers, KV, Workers AI) for the AI assistant. These providers may host data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Sharing with them is limited to the purposes above, and each of them is bound by its own data-processing terms to protect your data with the same or an equivalent level of protection as described in this policy. Transfers abroad rely on the safeguards these providers offer (such as standard contractual clauses and, where applicable, the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) and are carried out in line with KVKK Article 9. We do not sell personal data and we do not use your data for tracking or advertising.

7. Data retention

We keep personal data only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law:

  • registered email and cloud-synced presets: until you delete your account (or ask us to);
  • website launch-list email: until the launch campaign ends or you ask us to remove it;
  • AI assistant messages: not stored by us; rate-limit counters expire within 24 hours;
  • email-delivery logs at Resend: according to Resend's standard log retention;
  • purchase records: kept by Apple and RevenueCat under their own policies;
  • data on your device: until you remove it or delete the app.

Account deletion is described in section 8.

8. Account deletion

You can delete your account at any time from the Account screen in the app; you do not need to contact us. In-app deletion removes your synced saved calculations and your registered email from our sign-up records (Google Form responses and Google Sheets) and clears the account and saved calculations on this device. If the server-side deletion does not complete (for example, while you are offline), the app tells you and you can retry; you can also email team@varyosweld.com and we will complete the request within 30 days. Email-delivery logs held by our email provider expire under its own retention schedule. To remove an address you left on the website launch list, email us at the same address. Deleting your account does not cancel an App Store subscription; manage or cancel it in your Apple ID subscription settings (the Account screen links there). Saved PDF reports (the in-app Saved Reports list, up to 20), custom base and filler materials, and the AI assistant's random identifier and consent flags stay on the device until you remove the items individually or delete the app. Your name, if you entered one, was never transmitted or stored server-side, so there is nothing server-side to delete for it. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat are managed under their own policies.

9. Your rights (KVKK Article 11)

Under KVKK Article 11 you have the right to contact us and:

  • learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • request information if it has been processed,
  • learn the purpose of processing and whether it is used accordingly,
  • know the third parties to whom your data is transferred, in Turkey or abroad,
  • request correction if data is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • request deletion or destruction of your data under the conditions set by KVKK,
  • request that these actions be notified to third parties to whom data was transferred,
  • object to an outcome against you resulting solely from automated analysis,
  • claim compensation if you suffer damage due to unlawful processing.

If the GDPR applies to you, you also have the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with your local data-protection supervisory authority. In Türkiye you may also apply to the Personal Data Protection Board (KVKK Kurulu).

To exercise these rights, write to team@varyosweld.com. We respond within 30 days.

10. Children's privacy

The Service is a professional engineering tool not directed at children.

11. Changes to this policy

If our data practices change, we will update this policy and the app store listing will reflect the change. The current version is always published on this page.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, including account deletion requests, contact: team@varyosweld.com

This text is provided for general information and is not legal advice.

Gizlilik Politikası ve KVKK Aydınlatma Metni

Son güncelleme: 21 Ekim 2026

Varyos Weld, Türkiye Cumhuriyeti'nde yerleşik bireysel bir geliştirici (App Store sayfasında satıcı olarak görünen Muhammet Yiğit) tarafından işletilmektedir. Kişisel verileriniz, veri sorumlusu sıfatıyla bu geliştirici tarafından, aşağıda açıklanan kapsam ve amaçlarla işlenmektedir. Erişim, düzeltme veya silme talepleri dahil tüm sorularınız için team@varyosweld.com adresinden bize ulaşabilirsiniz.

2. İşlenen Kişisel Veriler

  • Web sitesi lansman listesi e-postası: Lansman bildirimi almak için siteye e-posta adresinizi bırakırsanız yalnızca e-posta adresiniz kaydedilir (Google Forms / Google Sheets üzerinde).
  • Kayıt verisi (isteğe bağlı): Uygulamada kayıt olursanız adınız ve e-posta adresiniz alınır. Adınız yalnızca cihazınızda tutulur ve hiçbir yere gönderilmez. E-posta adresiniz, karşılama e-postası gönderebilmemiz için bir Google Form'a iletilir ve ayrıca bulutla eşitlenen kayıtlı hesaplamalarınızın tanımlayıcısı olarak kullanılır.

  • Cihaz üzerinde tutulan veri: Uygulama, oturumunuzu, tercihlerinizi ve kayıtlı verilerinizi hatırlamak için cihazınızda yerel depolama (örneğin web sürümünde localStorage) kullanır; bu veriler bizim sunucularımıza otomatik olarak aktarılmaz.
  • PDF raporları: Oluşturduğunuz raporlar doğrudan cihazınızda oluşturulur ve yerel paylaş/yazdır menüsü ile paylaşılır. Rapor oluşturma sırasında hiçbir şey bir sunucuya yüklenmez. Raporlar cihazda uygulamadaki Kayıtlı Raporlar listesinde tutulur.

Ödeme bilgilerinizi almayız; e-posta adresinizi veya adınızı RevenueCat'e göndermeyiz.
  • Yapay zeka asistanı mesajları: bir sonraki bölüme bakınız.

3. Uygulama İçi Yapay Zeka Asistanı

Uygulamada, uygulamanın kendi hesaplayıcılarıyla ilgili soruları yanıtlayan isteğe bağlı bir yapay zeka asistanı bulunur. İlk kullanımdan önce uygulama, mesajlarınızı hangi hizmetin işlediğini ve nelerin gönderildiğini gösterir ve açık izninizi ister ("Kabul et ve devam et" veya "Şimdi değil"). Onay vermeden hiçbir şey gönderilmez. Bu izni Hesap ekranından istediğiniz zaman geri alabilirsiniz. Asistanı kullanırsanız:

  • O oturumdaki sohbetin o ana kadarki metni (mesajlarınız ve asistanın önceki yanıtları) ile uygulama diliniz, HTTPS üzerinden Cloudflare Workers üzerinde işlettiğimiz küçük bir aracı hizmete gönderilir. Bu hizmet, yanıt üretmek için sohbeti, Meta Llama 3.3 70B büyük dil modelini Cloudflare altyapısında çalıştıran Cloudflare Workers AI'a iletir. Asistan; hesaplama girdilerinizi, kayıtlı verilerinizi, e-posta adresinizi veya adınızı otomatik olarak almaz — yalnızca sohbete yazdıklarınız gönderilir. Lütfen sohbette hassas kişisel bilgi paylaşmayın.
  • Sohbet uygulamada bellekte tutulur ve oturumlar arasında saklanmaz. Mesajlarınızın içeriğini kendimiz saklamayız. Cloudflare, istekleri hizmet sağlayıcımız olarak işler; Cloudflare'in Workers AI gizlilik koşullarına göre istem ve yanıtlar isteğin işlenmesi dışında saklanmaz ve model eğitiminde kullanılmaz.
  • Kötüye kullanımı sınırlamak ve ücretsiz kullanım limitlerini korumak için aracı hizmet; uygulamanın oluşturup cihazınızda saklıyor olduğu rastgele bir tanımlayıcı başına, IP adresi başına ve genel olarak istekleri sayar. Bu sayaçlar kısa ömürlüdür (saatlik veya günlük pencere sonunda, en geç 24 saatte sona erer) ve e-posta adresiniz veya adınızla ilişkilendirilmez. Bu işlemenin hukuki sebebi, hizmetin kötüye kullanımını önlemeye yönelik meşru menfaatimizdir.
